## 5. Services the app talks to

when your device cannot make a payment's proof itself, to the prover you choose — by default the

and the app's own notice say so before your first send. You may point the app at your own node
and your own prover, or at none. The public node and the public prover are run on a best-effort
basis for the test network: they may be rate-limited, paused, changed or withdrawn at any time
